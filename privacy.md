# Celuna — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

---

## The short version

Celuna works on your device, and by default your information stays there — you can use the whole app with **no account**, and nothing leaves your phone. Sharing the care is optional: if you create an account and invite your family into a shared **“Circle,”** the information you choose to share is synced and stored securely so everyone helping sees the same thing. We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone outside your own circle, and we never show ads.

---

## Using Celuna without an account

If you never sign in, everything you enter is stored only in Celuna’s private storage on your device and is never transmitted. You only leave this mode by deliberately signing in and choosing to share.

---

## What Celuna stores on your device

- Profile names you create
- Daily check-ins, symptom tags, and notes
- Medications and reminder schedules
- Care tracking entries, including manually entered blood pressure and blood sugar readings
- Voice recordings and photos you choose to add
- Appointments and documents you add
- Your private journal and wellbeing entries (always kept to yourself — see below)

---

## Accounts

To share a Circle, you sign in with **Sign in with Apple**. Apple gives Celuna an anonymous account identifier and, only if you allow it, your name. There is no password for us to see, and you can hide your email from us. We keep only that identifier and the display name you allow.

---

## What is shared in a Circle

When you create or join a Circle, this is synced to the other members so everyone sees the same, current information:

- Details about the person being cared for
- Medications, schedules, and what has been given
- Appointments, the care team, and contacts
- The daily check, the care tracker, and blood pressure / blood sugar readings
- Notes, handoff information, and check-ins
- Behaviour moments you record
- Voice memories and documents you add
- The shared family chat (including any photos you attach), tasks, and care-shift schedule

This is stored on our servers (hosted by **Supabase**, in the United States) and is protected so that only members of your own circle can read it.

**What is never shared:** your private journal and your wellbeing / self-care entries are never synced to the circle or made visible to anyone else. They stay yours alone.

---

## Subscriptions

Caregiver+ is an optional subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your card or payment details. **RevenueCat** helps us know whether a subscription is active, tied to your account identifier. That is all it is used for.

---

## Services we use

- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, and App Store payments
- **Supabase** — the database and file storage for your shared circle, and the sign-in behind it
- **RevenueCat** — tracking whether your subscription is active

We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

---

## What we collect

Without an account, **nothing**. With an account and a shared Circle, we store your account identifier and the information you choose to share, as described above — and nothing more. Celuna has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising identifiers, ever.

---

## Health information

Some information you record may relate to health. It stays on your device unless you sign in and share a Circle (where it is visible only to your circle) or you choose to export or share it yourself. Anything you share by email, message, printing, or AirDrop leaves Celuna’s control and is governed by that service’s own policy.

---

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — only when you record a memory
- **Camera** — only when you photograph a document
- **Photo Library** — only when you attach a photo
- **Calendar** — only when you add an appointment to your own calendar
- **Notifications** — only for local reminders you set

Each is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

---

## Children

Celuna is not directed at children under 13.

---

## Your control and deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time. If you have an account, you can permanently delete it and everything synced to our servers from within Celuna (the account menu → **Delete account**), or by emailing us. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Information you added to a shared Circle may remain visible to the other members who still have it, the way a sent message stays in the recipient’s copy.

---

## Your rights

If you have an account, you can access, correct, export, or delete your information — most of it directly in the app, and the rest by emailing us. If you use Celuna without an account, everything is on your device and under your control.

---

## Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated date. Any genuinely new sharing of your information will remain something you choose, not something switched on quietly.
