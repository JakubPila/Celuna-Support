# Celuna Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

## The short version

Celuna stores your information on your device. We have no server that receives it. We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not show ads.

## What Celuna stores on your device

- Profile names you create
- Daily check-ins, symptom tags, and notes
- Medications and reminder schedules
- Care tracking entries, including manually entered blood pressure readings
- Voice recordings and photos you choose to add
- Appointments and documents you add

## What we collect

Nothing. Celuna has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no account server.

## Health information

Information you record may relate to health. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it. Anything you share by email, message, printing, or AirDrop leaves Celuna's control and is governed by that service's own policy.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — only when you record a memory
- **Photo Library** — only when you attach a photo
- **Notifications** — only for local reminders you set

Each is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

## Children

Celuna is not directed at children under 13.

## Your control and deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time. To remove everything, use Settings → Delete profile and all data. Deleting the app also permanently removes all local data. We hold no copy and cannot recover it.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You control everything on your device.

## Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated date. If a future version transmits data, we will update this policy and notify you in the app before it takes effect.
