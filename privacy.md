# Celuna — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

---

## The short version

Celuna works on your device, and by default your information stays there — you can use the whole app with **no account**, and nothing you enter leaves your phone. If you subscribe to Caregiver+, or invite your family into a shared **“Circle,”** your records are synced and stored securely — so they come back on a new phone, and everyone helping sees the same thing. We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone outside your own circle, and we never show ads.

---

## Using Celuna without an account

If you never sign in, everything you enter is stored only in Celuna’s private storage on your device, and Celuna never transmits it. You only leave this mode by deliberately signing in and choosing to share. Two small things are sent from every copy of Celuna, account or not, and neither is anything you enter: an anonymous identifier that lets App Store purchases work, and which Apple ad, if any, led to the download. Both are explained below, under **Subscriptions** and **Which ad brought you**.

---

## What Celuna stores on your device

- Profile names you create
- Daily check-ins, symptom tags, and notes
- Medications and reminder schedules
- Care tracking entries, including manually entered blood pressure and blood sugar readings
- Voice recordings and photos you choose to add
- Appointments and documents you add
- Your private journal and wellbeing entries (always kept to yourself — see below)

Your device’s own backups — iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer — include what Celuna stores there, your private journal too, so a new or restored phone picks up where the old one left off. Those backups are yours: they are made and kept under your Apple ID and your settings, and Apple’s privacy policy covers them. You can leave Celuna out of iCloud Backup in your iPhone’s Settings.

---

## Accounts

To subscribe to Caregiver+ or share a Circle, you sign in with **Sign in with Apple**. Apple gives Celuna an anonymous account identifier and, only if you allow it, your name. There is no password for us to see, and you can hide your email from us. We keep only that identifier and the display name you allow.

---

## What is shared in a Circle

When you create or join a Circle, this is synced to the other members so everyone sees the same, current information:

- Details about the person being cared for
- Medications, schedules, and what has been given
- Appointments, the care team, and contacts
- The daily check, the care tracker, and blood pressure / blood sugar readings
- Notes, handoff information, and check-ins
- Behaviour moments you record
- Voice memories and documents you add
- The shared family chat (including any photos you attach), tasks, and care-shift schedule
- Who logged each dose, care-log entry, reading and moment, and when

This is stored on our servers (hosted by **Supabase**, in the United States) and is protected so that only members of your own circle can read it.

If you subscribe to Caregiver+ on your own, Celuna keeps your records in a circle of your own, with just you in it, so they come back on a new phone. They are stored the same way, and only you can read them until you invite someone.

**What is never shared:** your private journal and your wellbeing / self-care entries are never sent to our servers, never synced to the circle, and never made visible to anyone else. They stay yours alone.

---

## Alerts from your circle

If you share a Circle and allow notifications, Celuna can alert you when someone else in it gives a dose, writes in the chat, or logs a reading, a moment or something in the care log — you choose which in Settings → Notifications. To do this, your phone gives our server an address for alerts (a “push token”) along with your choices, and each alert — for example “Donepezil — Given 7:52 PM by Anna” — passes through Expo’s push service to Apple, who deliver it to your phone. Alerts go only to the other members of your circle. “Hide details on the lock screen” keeps medicine names, readings, moments and messages out of them. Signing out stops them, and deleting your account deletes the address.

## Subscriptions

Caregiver+ is an optional subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your card or payment details. **RevenueCat** helps us know whether a subscription is active. It knows your copy of Celuna by an identifier — your account identifier once you sign in, and an anonymous one before that — and receives your purchase history from the App Store. It is never given anything you enter in Celuna.

---

## Which ad brought you

We advertise Celuna in App Store search results with **Apple Ads**. To learn which of those ads work, when Celuna opens your iPhone gives RevenueCat a token from Apple, and RevenueCat asks Apple whether the download came from one of our ads — and if it did, which campaign, ad group and search keyword, and the country or region. That answer is kept with the same identifier as your subscription status, so we can count how many families each ad brought and how many of them went on to subscribe.

This is Apple’s own attribution, and it needs no permission pop-up: Celuna never asks to track you, never uses your device’s advertising identifier, and nothing you enter in Celuna is part of it. We use the answer only to decide where to advertise Celuna, and we don’t share it with anyone else.

---

## Services we use

- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, delivering notifications, and telling us which Apple ad, if any, led to a download
- **Supabase** — the database and file storage for your records and your circle, and the sign-in behind it
- **RevenueCat** — knowing whether your subscription is active, and which Apple ad, if any, led to the download
- **Expo** (with Apple’s notification service) — delivering alerts from your circle to your phone, if you share a Circle and allow notifications

Apart from these, Celuna uses no analytics or advertising services, shows no ads, and never tracks you across other companies’ apps or websites.

---

## What we collect

Without an account, **nothing you enter** — only the anonymous identifier and the answer about Apple ads described above. With an account and Caregiver+ or a shared Circle, we store your account identifier, your phone’s address for alerts if you allow them, and the information described above — and nothing more. Celuna never asks to track you and never uses advertising identifiers.

---

## Health information

Some information you record may relate to health. It stays on your device unless you subscribe to Caregiver+ or share a Circle (where it is visible only to you and your circle), or you choose to export or share it yourself. Anything you share by email, message, printing, or AirDrop leaves Celuna’s control and is governed by that service’s own policy. Celuna is not a medical device.

---

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — only when you record a memory
- **Camera** — only when you photograph a document
- **Photo Library** — only when you attach a photo
- **Calendar** — only when you add an appointment to your own calendar
- **Notifications** — for the reminders you set and, if you share a Circle, alerts when someone else in it logs something (you choose which in Settings)

Each is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

---

## Children

Celuna is not directed at children under 13.

---

## Your control and deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time. If you have an account, you can permanently delete it and everything synced to our servers from within Celuna (**Account & backup → Delete account**), or by emailing us. The record of your purchases that RevenueCat keeps, and the answer about Apple ads, aren’t on our servers: email us and we will have them deleted too. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Information you added to a shared Circle may remain visible to the other members who still have it, the way a sent message stays in the recipient’s copy.

---

## Your rights

If you have an account, you can access, correct, export, or delete your information — most of it directly in the app, and the rest by emailing us. If you use Celuna without an account, everything you enter is on your device and under your control.

---

## Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated date. Any genuinely new sharing of your information will remain something you choose, not something switched on quietly.
