# Celuna Support

Celuna is a calm place for families caring for a loved one with dementia.

## Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback — email **celunaapp@gmail.com**
I usually reply within 2 business days.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device. Celuna has no server and doesn't collect your information.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Account & Backup → Delete profile and all data. Deleting the app also removes everything permanently.

**Can I use Celuna for someone who doesn't have dementia?**
Yes. It works for any caregiving situation.

**How do I report a bug?**
Email the address above with your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.
