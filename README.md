# Celuna-Support

Caring for someone with dementia means holding a hundred details in your head. Celuna gives you one calm place to keep them — daily check-ins, medications, care tracking, and voice memories. Turn what you log into a clear summary for the doctor. Everything works on your own device, and you can optionally share a private Circle so the whole family stays in sync — your information is never sold or used for ads. Not a medical device; consult a doctor for medical decisions.
